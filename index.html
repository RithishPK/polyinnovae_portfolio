the question

What makes you want to build like a polymath?

What reshaped how I build wasn't technical. After months of dodging fake internship offers and watching classmates get scammed, I understood the con isn't a code exploit — it's a psychology exploit. Scammers weaponize urgency, greed, and the fear of missing a rare opening, and technical literacy won't protect you if you can't see the manipulation. That reframed the problem: to detect scams I had to think like a behavioral analyst, not just an engineer. It's what pushed me to build ScamShield around manipulation patterns rather than keywords. I build best when I'm borrowing a lens from outside the code.
